Revenue Grid

A replacement for the shared Excel file that is the system of record for SPNI ad-sales revenue projections, across all six genres.

Eight screens, live and clickable. Tabs switch, filters recompute, toggles change the numbers.

Screen

Entry grid

Where a sales exec works. Every template column for the selected genre, with the calculated ones view only. Each exec sees their own clients and nothing else.

2 views

Manager View

What a sales manager sees: their region and their team, and a daily summary of everything the team changed. Email delivery of that summary is phase 2.

Screen

Projection summary

The monthly artifact: the SD Only, SD + HD and wallet share blocks, plus the impact property strip, by region.

Screen

Client tracking

Meeting coverage, the client funnel from TV down to SET and SAB, and the Client Type mix.

Screen

Coverage and accuracy

Meetings per exec per week, projected against actual, and the called-it / missed-it matrix once actuals are loaded.

2 views

PRS Insights and Masters

Everything PRS controls in one place: the entries that look off, and the lists, hierarchy and masters behind every dropdown.

Screen

Leadership dashboard

Filter by genre, region, client status and client type, then break the projection down by any of those plus group head and exec.

Screen

Delegation

Moving a client to another exec: permanent transfer when someone leaves, temporary cover when someone is away, and who is allowed to do each.
